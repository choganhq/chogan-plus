Chogan Plus is Chogan's collection of small, finishable games — more than sixty — with one extra: play against a friend on their own phone, right next to you, with no internet.

Two phones
Open a two-player game, choose "Two phones", and the phones find each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi through Google Play services. Reversi plays this way today; more games follow with updates.

The games
Chess, Sudoku, Minesweeper, Backgammon, Hokm, Pasur, Mahjong, FreeCell, Spider, Klondike, 8-Ball Pool, Snooker, Air Hockey, Ping Pong, Go, Gomoku, Checkers, Reversi, Ludo, Dominoes, Tower Defence, Block Drop, Water Sort, Nonogram and many more. Each game has a daily challenge, awards and a weekly league.

What stays the same
* Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.
* No account and no internet permission. Your progress stays on your phone.
* Persian, English, Chinese, German, Spanish and Polish.
* Open source under the MIT licence: github.com/choganhq/chogan-plus

Chogan Plus is a separate app from Chogan and starts with fresh progress. Chogan itself stays on F-Droid and the web as before.
